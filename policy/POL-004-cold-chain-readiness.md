# POL-004 — Synthetic Cold-Chain Readiness Policy

> **Demo-only policy.** This document is synthetic and was created for the DLA Medical Supply Readiness Agent interview prototype. It is not an official DLA, DoD, or DHA policy.

## Purpose

Provide simple readiness guidance for synthetic medical items marked `cold_chain_required = true`.

## Handling requirement

Cold-chain items must remain within an approved temperature-controlled handling process during storage and transportation. A delay or cold-chain exception should be treated as a readiness concern until the affected material is confirmed usable.

## Shortage-risk interpretation

For a cold-chain item, late inbound units, backorders, or an active cold-chain supplier event should increase the urgency of readiness review because replacement or redistribution options may be more constrained than for routine ambient-storage items.

The agent must not infer that material affected by a cold-chain exception is usable. It should report the exception and recommend human validation of disposition.

## Contingency response

When a cold-chain item is near or below minimum stock, planners should prioritize:

1. validating the status and usability of inbound supply,
2. identifying other approved temperature-controlled inventory that may be transferable,
3. reviewing alternate approved sources capable of cold-chain fulfillment,
4. escalating the position when replenishment cannot restore the required minimum in time.

Any substitution, transfer, or procurement action requires authorized human approval.
