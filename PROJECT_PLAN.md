# DLA Medical Supply Readiness Agent — Project Plan

## 1. Project purpose

This repository supports the DataRobot Senior AI Solutions Engineer — Federal interview take-home assignment.

The goal is to build and present a technically credible MVP of an AI agent that solves a high-value Federal enterprise problem while remaining easy for a mixed technical/business audience to understand.

The selected scenario is a **DLA Medical Supply Readiness Agent** for **Defense Logistics Agency (DLA) Troop Support Medical**.

The solution demonstrates more than conversational RAG. It combines:

- large-scale structured operational data in Databricks,
- governed SQL/data-analysis capabilities,
- unstructured policy retrieval,
- a DataRobot predictive model for future shortage risk,
- agent orchestration in DataRobot,
- and a clear path toward simulation and human-approved actions.

All operational records generated by this repository are **synthetic**. Public DLA scale metrics are stored separately with provenance and must never be represented as generated operational facts. The policy corpus is also explicitly synthetic and must never be represented as official DLA, DoD, or DHA policy.

---

## 2. Business problem

DLA Troop Support Medical supports a large and operationally critical medical supply chain. Supply planners must anticipate shortages while reconciling inventory, demand, open orders, supplier lead times, shipment delays, backorders, expiration risk, mission priority, readiness requirements, and governing policy.

The underlying information spans three information types:

1. **Structured operational data** — inventory, orders, shipments, suppliers, customers, consumption, and historical performance.
2. **Unstructured governing context** — medical supply policies, stocking requirements, contingency guidance, and sourcing procedures.
3. **Predictive risk** — a DataRobot model-generated estimate of whether a currently acceptable supply position will fall below minimum stock within the next 30 days.

### Million-dollar question

> **Which critical medical supplies are at risk of shortage over the next 30 days, where will those shortages occur, why, and what should we do about them?**

This question anchors the data model, predictive model, tools, agent, and live demo.

Opening line:

> **A shortage shouldn't be discovered when the shelf is empty.**

The primary persona is a **DLA medical supply planner / readiness analyst** preparing for an operational readiness review.

The experience should feel like an analytical teammate that can use governed tools, not a chatbot guessing from text.

The final demo interface is the **DataRobot Agentic Application Starter** backed by the deployed Readiness Agent. This is the preferred customer-facing demo surface.

Representative questions include:

- Which medical supply positions have the highest predicted shortage risk over the next 30 days?
- Why is the top position at risk?
- Which suppliers have the weakest recent delivery performance?

- Which critical items are most at risk, why, and what should we do about them?

## 5. Final MVP architecture

```text
User
  |
  v
DataRobot Agentic Application Starter
  |
  v
DataRobot Readiness Agent / Orchestrator
  |
  +------------------------------+-----------------------------+
  |                              |                             |
  v                              v                             v
Tool 1                         Tool 2                        Tool 3
Operational Analyst           Policy RAG                   Predictive Risk Analyst
Databricks Operational        DataRobot-native RAG         Thin NAT adapter
Genie                         + vector database             |
  |                              |                             v
  | Managed Genie MCP            |                       Databricks Genie
  v                              v                       Conversation API
Operational Delta tables      Synthetic policy corpus          |
                                                               v
                                                     Medical Supply Forecast Genie
                                                               |
                                                               v
                                              agent_shortage_risk_predictions
                                                               |
                                                               v
                                              DataRobot batch prediction output
```

Future production tools: scenario simulation and human-approved action creation.

### Architectural principles

> **The agent reasons. Tools analyze. The predictive model predicts.**

> **LLMs do not analyze terabytes of raw operational data. Tools do.**

> **The Operational Genie explains what is happening. RAG explains what rules apply. The Prediction Genie analyzes what the DataRobot model says is likely to happen.**

> **The prediction Genie does not predict. It slices, ranks, and summarizes DataRobot model output.**

The three MVP tools intentionally have separate responsibilities. DataRobot training/scoring/prediction tables remain excluded from Tool 1's Operational Genie. Tool 3 gets its own prediction-only Genie over a curated serving view whose probability column comes directly from authoritative DataRobot batch prediction output.

## 6. Tool plan and final MVP status

### Tool 1 — Operational Data Analyst

**Status: ✅ COMPLETE AND WORKING**

Implementation:

- Databricks Operational Genie
- exposed through Databricks managed MCP
- connected to DataRobot through the starter's existing external MCP configuration

Responsibility:

> **What is happening and why?**

Operational scope includes:

- customers / facilities / locations,
- medical items,
- inventory and days of supply,
- orders and requisitions,
- shipments and late deliveries,
- backorders,
- suppliers and supplier events,
- supplier delivery performance,
- current supply position,
- historical operational trends.

Prediction assets remain explicitly outside Tool 1.

Validated behavior includes:

- counting the 120 represented customer locations,
- ranking low-days-of-supply positions,
- supplier performance analysis,
- multi-table operational analysis from the deployed runtime.

### Tool 2 — Policy / Contract RAG

**Status: ✅ COMPLETE AND WORKING**

Implementation:

- synthetic Markdown policy corpus,
- DataRobot-resident vector database,
- DataRobot RAG LLM blueprint,
- deployed blueprint exposed to the top-level agent as `policy_rag`.

Responsibility:

> **What policy, stocking rule, or escalation guidance applies?**

Working corpus:

- `POL-001-medical-stocking-and-readiness.md`
- `POL-002-shortage-escalation-and-emergency-sourcing.md`
- `POL-003-supplier-performance-escalation.md`
- `POL-004-cold-chain-readiness.md`

Validated behavior includes stocking thresholds, emergency sourcing, supplier escalation, cold-chain handling, and source-grounded responses.

### Tool 3 — Predictive Risk Analyst

**Status: ✅ COMPLETE AND WORKING**

Implementation:

- authoritative DataRobot shortage-risk model and batch predictions,
- curated Databricks view `agent_shortage_risk_predictions`,
- dedicated **Medical Supply Forecast Genie** over that serving view,
- thin DataRobot NAT tool `prediction_genie`,
- Databricks Genie Conversation API integration.

The final Tool 3 design deliberately **does not provision a second MCP client or add new DataRobot runtime-parameter infrastructure**. The custom NAT adapter calls the dedicated Forecast Genie directly and reuses the already-working Databricks authorization configuration used for Tool 1.

Responsibility:

> **What does the DataRobot model predict, and how are those predictions distributed across the supply network?**

The probability field is:

`shortage_probability_30d`

It is derived directly from the DataRobot batch prediction output column:

`shortage_within_30d_1_PREDICTION`

Tool 3 may:

- filter predictions,
- rank highest/lowest-risk positions,
- group by approved descriptive dimensions,
- calculate average/max risk across groups,
- count positions over user-specified thresholds.

Tool 3 may not:

- generate or recalculate probabilities,
- infer operational root cause,
- answer policy questions,
- imply that group-level average probability is a causal probability for a supplier, region, or customer.

### Full three-tool orchestration

**Status: ✅ COMPLETE FOR DEMO**

The top-level Readiness Agent now routes among all three specialized capabilities and works well through the Agentic Application Starter.

Expected routing:

```text
"What does the model predict?"
→ Tool 3

"Why is this happening?"
→ Tool 1

"What policy applies?"
→ Tool 2

"Which items are most at risk, why, and what should we do?"
→ Tool 3 → Tool 1 → Tool 2 → orchestrated recommendation
```

The current user experience is considered strong enough for the live interview demo.

ML:

- `ml_shortage_predictions_raw`
- `agent_shortage_risk_predictions`

### DataRobot shortage-risk model

**Status: ✅ COMPLETE**

Target:

`shortage_within_30d`

Conceptually:

```text
P(fall below minimum stock threshold in next 30 days | information available today)
```

The MVP champion is a Light Gradient Boosted Trees classifier with early stopping.

Approximate validated performance after correcting the synthetic end-of-history distribution shift:

- Backtest PR AUC: ~0.85
- Holdout PR AUC: ~0.82

Batch scoring path:

```text
Databricks ml_shortage_scoring
        ↓
DataRobot deployed champion
        ↓
DataRobot batch prediction job
        ↓
Databricks ml_shortage_predictions_raw
        ↓
agent_shortage_risk_predictions
```

## 11. Intended live-demo sequence

Opening prompt:

> **I'm preparing for tomorrow's medical supply readiness review. Which critical medical supplies concern you most over the next 30 days, where, why, and what should I do about them?**

Expected behavior:

1. **Tool 3 / Prediction Genie** identifies and ranks authoritative DataRobot 30-day shortage risk.
2. **Tool 1 / Operational Genie** investigates current operational evidence and root cause for the selected risk.
3. **Tool 2 / Policy RAG** retrieves applicable stocking/readiness/escalation guidance.
4. The Readiness Agent synthesizes an executive recommendation while keeping prediction, observed evidence, and policy distinct.

Potential follow-ups:

- **Why is the top position at risk?**
- **How has that supplier performed recently?**
- **What stocking requirement applies here?**
- **Which suppliers are associated with the greatest number of positions above 70% predicted shortage risk?**
- **Compare average and maximum predicted shortage risk across customer regions.**

Desired demo story:

> **Predict → Explain → Govern → Recommend**

## 12. Presentation messages to preserve

### Business value

> **A shortage shouldn't be discovered when the shelf is empty.**

The agent changes the planner workflow from reactive shortage discovery to proactive, evidence-grounded readiness analysis.

### Big-data architecture

> **LLMs do not analyze terabytes of raw operational data. Tools do.**

Structured analytics execute where the data lives and return compact governed results for reasoning.

### Predictive AI

> **The agent reasons. DataRobot's predictive model predicts.**

The DataRobot model owns predictive inference. The Forecast Genie only analyzes existing model output.

### Tool specialization

> **The Operational Genie explains what is happening. RAG explains what rules apply. The Prediction Genie analyzes what the DataRobot model says is likely to happen.**

### Federal governance

The architecture naturally supports discussion of:

- least privilege,
- read-only analytical tools,
- approved schemas,
- identity propagation,
- source attribution,
- model lineage / observability,
- auditability,
- human approval for consequential actions,
- deterministic controls around model reasoning.

### MVP scope

The demo intentionally stops at decision support.

Future production extensions include:

- deterministic scenario simulation,
- human-approved inventory transfer / sourcing actions,
- formal agent evaluation suites,
- drift and performance monitoring,
- hardened identity propagation,
- additional latency/load testing.
